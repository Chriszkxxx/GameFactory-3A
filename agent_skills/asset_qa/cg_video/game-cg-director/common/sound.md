# Sound

- Time speech, singing, visible devices, and action sounds to playback. Tie action sound to a visible or established offscreen source; sound alone cannot stand in for a scene-defining participant.
- Cover ambience, physical action sounds, and non-verbal human sounds without duplicating spoken words.
- When audience-only music is requested, give it one functional arc tied to the visible beats—establish, build, peak or cut, and resolve as applicable. Describe instrumentation, tempo, rhythm, and audible dynamic change without inventing a genre from mood alone.
- Keep diegetic and non-diegetic audio distinct and consistent with the visible timeline.
- Give the primary gameplay event the clearest audible peak; keep secondary layers lower so its source and result remain identifiable.
- Follow the selected model profile for field names, placement, and the notation for silence or absent music.
