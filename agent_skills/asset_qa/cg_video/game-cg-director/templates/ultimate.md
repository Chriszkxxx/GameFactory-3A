# Ultimate template

Present a signature ability whose user, space, function, power, and requested outcome remain readable at full intensity.

- Choose the dramatic function from the intent: activation or character reveal, attack and result, clash, or powered face-off. Do not invent contact, damage, or a second attack when the request only establishes power.
- Before effects expand, reveal the user, target or affected area when present, facing, power source, and intended path or boundary.
- Preserve cue or preparation → activation or release → visible proof of power → requested reaction, aftermath, or end pose. Include travel and contact only when the ability reaches a target.
- Let effects show where power begins, travels or occupies space, and what it changes. Secondary particles must not hide body performance, boundaries, contact, or reaction.
- Match visual and audio hierarchy to importance: cue before action, peak at activation, release, or impact, then recede to reveal the result.
- Preserve screen direction and character geography. Move the camera to reveal body mechanics, ability behavior, or changed state, then return to a readable relationship when another character remains relevant.
- Give multiple performers separate phases and speaker IDs; extend duration within model limits instead of overlapping complete speeches and ability performances.
