# Opening template

Introduce the game world, its behavior, and what deserves attention next. Treat the opening as playable exposition, not a beauty reel.

- Show genre and world through observable activity.
- Choose shots and cuts by visual tasks, spatial clarity, action continuity, available time, and mode.
- Combine space and activity when readable; cut when a new viewpoint clarifies space, action, or focus.
- Use a landmark, route, boundary, objective, or moving subject as the spatial anchor; preserve it or the defining activity when continuity matters.
- End with the focal subject, direction, destination, or conflict readable.

Do not invent a title, narrator, lore statement, or unrelated final panorama.
