# Mechanic Code Generation Requirements — gameForge_fps

## Overview
Generate complete UE5 gameplay logic (Blueprints or C++ module) for the
first-person tactical shooter. The generated code receives weapon assets,
enemy character assets, animation clips, and the scene as input.

## Engine
- **Engine**: Unreal Engine 5
- **Module Name**: FPSGameplay

## Input Assets
- **Weapon Model**: shotgun (generated via Meshy API, pump-action, PBR)
- **Enemy Model**: terrorist (all black clothing, generated via Meshy API)
- **Animation Clips**:
  - Enemy: idle, walk, shoot, hit_react, death (5 clips)
  - Player hands: shotgun_idle, shotgun_shoot, shotgun_reload (3 clips)
- **Scene**: Imported open-source abandoned prison map
- **VFX Assets**: muzzle_flash, blood_splash, spark_impact
- **Engine API Docs**: agent_skills/engine_context/ue5_api.md

## Required Systems

### 1. FPSPlayerController
First-person movement and look.
- WASD movement (walk)
- Shift to sprint
- Space to jump
- Ctrl to crouch
- Mouse controls camera (pitch + yaw)
- CharacterController physics
- Head bob effect when moving

### 2. WeaponSystem
Shotgun firing, reload, and recoil.
- Left click fires shotgun
- Shotgun pellet spread (6-8 pellets per shot, random spread angle)
- High close-range damage, falloff at distance
- Pump-action reload animation
- R key triggers reload
- Recoil: camera kick + weapon model kick
- Muzzle flash VFX on each shot
- Ammo: 6 shells per magazine, reserve ammo
- Fire rate: pump action, ~1 shot per second

### 3. FPSHitSystem
Hit detection and damage.
- Raycast per pellet for shotgun spread
- Headshot: 2x damage multiplier
- Body: normal damage
- Limbs: 0.7x damage
- Blood splash VFX on enemy hit
- Bullet hole decals on environment
- Damage values:
  - Shotgun pellet body hit: 15 damage per pellet
  - Shotgun pellet headshot: 30 damage per pellet

### 4. EnemyAI
Enemy behavior state machine.
- Patrol: walks predefined route when player undetected
- Alert: detects player via sight/hearing, moves to engage
- Engage: shoots at player, strafes
- Cover: moves to nearest cover when taking fire
- State transitions based on player distance and line of sight
- Enemy health: 100 HP
- Enemy weapon: rifle, deals 8 damage per hit
- Fire rate: 1 shot per 1.5 seconds

### 5. EnemySpawner
Enemy spawn management.
- Pre-placed enemies in the prison map
- Optional wave-based reinforcement
- Tracks alive enemies for win condition

### 6. FPSDamageSystem
Player health and damage feedback.
- Player health: 100 HP
- Damage from enemy fire reduces health
- Screen-edge red vignette when taking damage
- Health regenerates slowly after 3 seconds without damage
- Death at 0 HP triggers game over

### 7. FPSHUDController
HUD interaction.
- Crosshair with shotgun spread indicator
- Ammo count display
- Health indicator
- Objective text
- Kill feed

## Acceptance Criteria
- Compile success
- Player spawns with shotgun visible in first person
- WASD moves player, mouse controls view
- Left click fires shotgun with muzzle flash and spread
- R reloads shotgun with pump animation
- Enemies spawn and patrol
- Enemies detect and shoot at player
- Shooting enemy causes damage, hit reaction, blood VFX
- Headshot causes 2x damage
- Enemy death animation plays (fall forward/backward)
- All enemies killed → mission complete screen
- Player takes damage, red vignette appears
- Player death → game over screen
- Crosshair, ammo, health, objective visible
- Kill notification appears
- Restart works

## Validation Checks
1. compile_success — Game compiles without errors
2. player_can_move — WASD + mouse look works
3. weapon_fires — Shotgun fires with muzzle flash and spread
4. reload_works — R triggers reload animation
5. enemy_spawns — Enemies present in scene
6. enemy_ai_works — Enemies patrol, detect, engage
7. hit_detection — Bullets damage enemies
8. headshot_multiplier — Headshots deal 2x damage
9. enemy_death — Death animation plays
10. player_takes_damage — Health decreases from enemy fire
11. mission_complete — All enemies killed triggers win
12. game_over — Player death triggers loss
13. hud_updates — Ammo, health, crosshair update
14. kill_notification — Kill feed shows on enemy death
