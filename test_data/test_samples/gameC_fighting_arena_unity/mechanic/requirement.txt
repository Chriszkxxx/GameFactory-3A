# Mechanic Code Generation Requirements — gameC_fighting_arena_unity

## Overview
Generate a complete Unity3D C# Assembly (DLL) containing all gameplay
mechanics for the 3D side-scrolling versus fighting game. The generated code
is an outer-Agent task that receives character prefabs, animation clips, scene,
and VFX assets as input, then produces a compiled Unity Assembly with all
required gameplay scripts.

## Engine
- **Engine**: Unity3D 2022.3 URP
- **Assembly**: FightingArenaGameplay.dll
- **Module Name**: FightingArenaGameplay

## Input Assets
- **Character Prefabs**: warrior.prefab, ninja.prefab (2 fighters)
- **Animation Clips**: 7 core animations per character (14 total)
  - idle, move, light_punch, heavy_punch, kick, block, ultimate
  - light and heavy kick gameplay may share the same generated kick clip
  - non-core runtime states use the current pose or a mechanic-only fallback
- **Scene**: FightingArena.unity (Japanese stone arena)
- **VFX Assets**: fire_punch.prefab, impact_shockwave.prefab,
  blade_slash_trail.prefab, block_shield.prefab
- **Audio Assets**: dialogue WAVs, SFX WAVs
- **Engine API Docs**: agent_skills/engine_context/unity3d_api.md

## Static Preview / Motion Late Binding

The first Unity preview is assembled before generated motion is available.
For this milestone, character prefabs and the arena must still be imported and
the complete movement, combat, round, AI, camera, and UI logic must run.
Animator controllers and animation clips are optional: when they are absent,
keep each imported character in its source static pose and drive locomotion by
the character root transform. Attack timing, hit detection, health, energy,
round state, and UI updates must not depend on animation events.

Do not generate fake animation files. Keep named animation binding slots for
the final motion set so later FBX clips can be attached without regenerating
or rewriting the core mechanic assembly.

Core motion binding uses `MoveSpeed` (float), `MoveForward` (float),
`MoveDepth` (float), `FighterState` (int), `LightPunch`, `HeavyPunch`,
`LightKick`, `HeavyKick`, `Ultimate` (triggers), and `Blocking` (bool). Idle
and block are stationary loops, move is an in-place loop, and the character
controller owns all root movement. Prop mechanics do not require dedicated
generated character motions.

## Required Scripts (Classes)

### 1. FightingCharacterController
Character control for movement, attack, and block.
- Horizontal movement (A/D, ←/→)
- Depth movement (W/S, ↑/↓) — limited to ~5 meter range
- Platform-aware gravity and landing on every marked arena surface
- Double jump from the ground or air
- Ground dash plus one air dash per airtime, with cooldown and bounded travel
- Prevent ground movement through the vertical sides of raised platforms
- Character always faces opponent
- Attack input → play attack animation → activate hitbox during active frames
- Block input → play block animation → reduce incoming damage
- Hit reaction triggered by combat system
- Knockdown/getup state transitions
- Ultimate skill activation when energy is full
- Pick up the nearest available stone lantern, carry it while moving, throw
  it toward the opponent, and drop it safely on death or round reset

### 2. FightingInputHandler
Input routing for P1 (keyboard) and P2 (keyboard or AI).
- P1: A/D (move), W/S (depth), J (light punch), K (heavy punch),
  L (light kick), O (heavy kick), Space (jump/double jump), Left Shift
  (ground/air dash), U (block), I (ultimate)
- P2: ←/→ (move), ↑/↓ (depth), Numpad 0 (jump), Numpad Enter
  (dash), Numpad 1/2 (punches), Numpad 3/6 (kicks), Numpad 4/5
  (block/ultimate)
- P1: E (pick up prop), F (throw held prop)
- P2: Numpad 7 (pick up prop), Numpad 8 (throw held prop)
- Routes input to the correct FightingCharacterController
- Supports AI takeover for P2

### 3. FightingCombatSystem
Hit detection, damage calculation, and hitstun frames.
- Hitbox/hurtbox overlap detection during attack active frames
- Fighter health: 200 HP
- Damage values per attack type:
  - Light punch: 3 damage, low hitstun
  - Heavy punch: 5 damage, medium hitstun
  - Light kick: 3 damage, medium reach
  - Heavy kick: 5 damage, long startup and high hitstun
  - Ultimate: 20 damage, launch effect
- Each continuous block negates its first three incoming hits; the fourth hit
  deals normal damage. Releasing and pressing block starts a new three-hit guard.
- Hitstun duration prevents action during reaction
- Knockdown triggered when HP < 20% on hit

### 4. FightingRoundManager
Round management: timer, win/loss, best-of-5.
- Round timer: 99 seconds countdown
- Round ends on K.O. (HP = 0) or timer expiry
- Timer expiry: fighter with more HP wins the round
- Best of 5: first to win 3 rounds wins the match
- Round transitions: "Round X", "FIGHT!", "K.O.", "You Win/Lose"
- Reset characters to start positions between rounds
- Match end screen with winner announcement

### 5. FightingCameraController
Side-view camera following the midpoint of both fighters.
- Fixed side angle (horizontal plane view)
- Follows midpoint between P1 and P2
- Zoom adjustment based on distance between fighters
- Smooth interpolation, no jitter
- Clamped to arena bounds

### 6. FightingComboSystem
Combo system for chained attacks.
- Light punch → heavy punch → flying kick (3-hit combo)
- Combo window: 0.3 seconds between inputs
- Combo counter displayed on screen
- Increased damage for successful combos
- Combo break on block, hit, or timeout

### 7. FightingUltimateSkill
Ultimate skill system with energy charge and CG trigger.
- Normal attacks add energy equal to actual damage dealt; blocked attacks add 0
- Ultimate available at 60 energy
- Activation: freeze time, play ultimate CG cinematic
- Warrior ultimate: Fire Punch (20 damage, flame VFX)
- Ninja ultimate: Blade Draw Slash (20 damage, blade VFX)
- Post-cinematic: resume combat with damage applied
- Energy resets to 0 after use

### 8. FightingAIBehavior
AI opponent behavior tree for P2.
- States: idle, approach, attack, defend, retreat, ultimate
- Decision tree based on distance, HP, energy:
  - Far: approach
  - Medium: attack or approach
  - Close: attack or block (randomized)
  - Low HP: retreat or block
  - Full energy: use ultimate
- Reaction time delay (0.2-0.5 seconds) for fairness
- Difficulty scaling (easy/medium/hard)

### 9. FightingThrowableProp
Reusable stage-prop gameplay.
- Imported mesh bounds create a stable root collider
- Idle prop is a solid arena obstacle
- Collider is disabled while carried or in flight
- Two-handed carry follows the fighter and uses named Animator parameters
- Throw follows a gravity arc toward the opponent
- One hit deals 18 damage and applies dedicated hit stun/reaction
- Landing on a marked arena surface restores collision and pickup eligibility
- Falling outside the arena resets the prop to its spawn point

## Acceptance Criteria
- Compile success (no CS errors)
- Characters can move with A/D and arrow keys
- W/S controls depth movement
- Fighters can jump onto and land on raised platforms
- Double jump, ground dash, and one air dash per airtime work
- Characters face each other at all times
- Light/heavy punches and light/heavy kicks have distinct timing, range, and damage
- Red arena/dojo columns have solid collision
- Stone lantern can be picked up with E, carried, thrown with F, damages the
  opponent, lands on arena surfaces, and can be picked up again
- Block reduces damage
- Hit reaction animations play on hit
- Knockdown triggered at low HP
- Timer counts down from 99
- K.O. ends round when HP reaches 0
- Best of 5, first to 3 wins works correctly
- Energy bar fills during combat
- Ultimate triggers VFX and deals damage
- HP bars update in real time
- Round indicator and score update across rounds 1-5
- FIGHT text appears at round start
- Press Enter on match end to restart

## Validation Checks
1. compile_success — Assembly compiles without errors
2. character_can_move — Characters respond to movement input
3. animation_can_play — All 7 core animations per character play correctly after generation and retarget
4. combat_deals_damage — Attacks reduce opponent HP
5. block_reduces_damage — Blocking mitigates damage
6. round_timer_works — Timer counts down from 99
7. ko_detected — Round ends on HP = 0
8. best_of_five — Match ends after 3 round wins
9. energy_fills — Energy bar accumulates during combat
10. ultimate_triggers — Ultimate skill activates VFX and damage
11. ui_updates — HP bars, timer, round indicator update
12. ai_works — AI opponent makes decisions and fights
13. camera_follows — Camera tracks both fighters
14. combo_system — Combo chains work with timing window
