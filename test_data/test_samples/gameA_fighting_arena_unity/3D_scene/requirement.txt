# 3D Scene Generation Requirements — gameA_fighting_arena_unity

## Overview
Generate a 3D scene of a Japanese stone fighting arena for the versus fighting
game. The scene is generated from a reference image and/or text prompt using
Hunyuan-WorldPlay2 or FlashWorld.

## Model Backend
- **Scene Generation**: Hunyuan-WorldPlay2 / FlashWorld (text/image → 3D scene)
- **Backend**: worldmirror (frames → mesh) or worldplay (image + prompt → frames → mesh)

## Scene Description
A traditional Japanese stone fighting arena set in an ancient courtyard:
- Circular or square stone fighting platform (擂台) with surrounding railings
- Compact multi-level layout: a clear main combat floor, two raised side
  platforms, and one shallow rear platform that can be reached by jumping
- Background features: torii gates (鳥居), stone lanterns (石灯籠), bamboo grove (竹林)
- Stone slab floor with visible crack details
- Center of the arena has a marking/pattern
- Warm color tones throughout
- Limited depth: ~5 meters of forward/backward movement range for fighters

## Lighting Requirements
- **CRITICAL**: Use even, uniform ambient lighting — minimize shadows
- Avoid strong directional light projections
- Characters must always be clearly visible from the side-view camera
- Warm ambient color temperature

## Generation Parameters
- Image path: arena_ref.png (reference image for scene)
- Prompt: "Japanese stone fighting arena with torii gates in background, stone lanterns, bamboo grove, flat even lighting minimal shadows, warm tones, game-ready 3D scene"
- Seed: 42
- Sky removal: enabled (outdoor scene, but sky should not dominate the mesh)
- Max offplane: 8.0 (standard culling)
- Close holes: 2 (pinhole closing)
- Num frames: 8

## Output Path
- test_data/outputs/gameA_fighting_arena_unity/<run_id>/assets/3d_scene/<task_id>/scene.glb

## Notes
- The scene should provide a clear, flat fighting surface at ground level
- Every playable platform must have continuous collision and enough clearance
  for two fighters; decorative props must not be treated as playable surfaces
- The layout must be tight and readable from a side-view camera, with no large
  empty gaps or randomly scattered distant structures
- Every visible rear building must have an open entrance, continuous interior
  floor, and wall collision so fighters can enter and continue combat inside
- Lanterns and bamboo remain decorative and non-collidable
- The arena should feel enclosed but not claustrophobic
- Stone textures should have realistic weathering and crack details
- Scene scale: 1 unit = 1 meter, arena diameter approximately 8-10 meters
