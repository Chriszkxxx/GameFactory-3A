# gameForge_fight — Game Design Brief

## Page 1: Concept & Gameplay

### Game Vision
gameForge_fight is a 3D one-on-one boxing/fighting game built on Unreal Engine 5.
Two fighters — a muscular warrior and an armored goddess — face off in an
open-air medieval arena. The game delivers visceral combat with punch combos,
energy-charged ultimates, and dramatic fire VFX moments.

### Core Gameplay Loop
Launch → P1/P2 Spawn → Fight (move, punch, block) → HP reaches 0 →
Round End → Best of 3 → Match End → Restart

Each match is best-of-3 rounds. Each round has a 99-second timer. When a
fighter's HP reaches 0, the round ends with a K.O. If the timer expires, the
fighter with more HP wins the round. The first fighter to win 2 rounds wins
the match.

### Camera & Perspective
Side-view camera positioned perpendicular to the fighting plane. The camera
follows the midpoint between both fighters and zoomes based on their distance.
Characters can move horizontally and in limited depth, always facing each other.

### Characters

**Character A — Warrior (武士)**
A bare-chested, heavily muscled warrior with arm bandages. His fighting style
is powerful and deliberate — heavy punches and an devastating ultimate. His
ultimate skill, the Fire Punch Uppercut (火焰上勾拳), engulfs his fist in flame
for a massive uppercut strike dealing heavy damage.

**Character B — Armored Goddess (铠甲女神)**
A female fighter wearing ornate armor. Her fighting style is agile and precise
— quick strikes and fluid movement. She shares the same ultimate mechanic:
the Fire Punch Uppercut, with identical fire VFX on her fist.

Both characters use the same combat system: regular punches charge energy,
and at full energy the ultimate fire uppercut becomes available.

### Combat System
- **Health**: Each fighter starts every round with 100 HP
- **Punch** (J/Numpad1): Regular forward punch, 5 damage, charges 10 energy
- **Block** (U/Numpad4): Reduces incoming damage
- **Ultimate** (I/Numpad5): Available at 100 energy, fire uppercut, 25 damage
- **Energy**: Only unblocked punches add energy; blocked punches add 0
- **Hit Reactions**: Stagger on hit, brief hitstun

### Scene
Imported open-source medieval exterior map (Sicka Yazd environment). Open-air
stone arena with medieval architecture, stone floor, and structural columns.
Lighting is ambient and even — fighters must always be clearly visible. Warm
color tones pervade the arena.

## Page 2: Technical & Production

### Engine & Architecture
- **Engine**: Unreal Engine 5
- **Code Module**: FightArenaGameplay (Blueprints or C++)
- **UI Module**: FightArenaUI (UMG widgets)
- **Asset Pipeline**: AI-generated assets imported into UE5

### AI Asset Generation Pipeline
1. **T-pose Images**: QwenEdit + RMBG → clean T-pose reference images
2. **3D Objects**: Meshy API → character GLB models
3. **Motions**: MoMask → BVH → Puppeteer rig + retarget → animation FBX (5 motions per character, 10 total)
4. **Scene**: Imported open-source Sicka Yazd medieval map
5. **Audio**: Seed Audio API (dialogue + SFX) → WAV files

### Game Mechanics (7 Systems)
1. FightCharacterController — movement, attack, block, state machine
2. FightInputHandler — P1/P2 keyboard input routing + AI toggle
3. FightCombatSystem — hitbox detection, damage, hitstun, energy charge
4. FightRoundManager — timer, K.O., best-of-3, match flow
5. FightCameraController — side-view midpoint tracking camera
6. FightUltimateSkill — energy charge, fire VFX trigger, ultimate damage
7. FightAIBehavior — AI opponent (approach/attack/retreat)

### UI Design
- HP bars (top-left/right) with portraits, flash white on damage
- Energy bars below HP, segmented, glow when full
- Round indicator and score (center top): current round, P1/P2 wins
- Timer (center): 99→0 countdown, red pulse at ≤10s
- Ultimate overlay: fire VFX screen effect
- Match end: winner name, "Press ENTER to Restart"

### VFX
- Punch impact: small particle burst
- Fire Punch Uppercut: flame ignition → flame trail → flame burst on impact

### Controls
| Action | P1 | P2 |
|--------|----|----|
| Move | A/D | ←/→ |
| Move Depth | W/S | ↑/↓ |
| Punch | J | Numpad 1 |
| Block | U | Numpad 4 |
| Ultimate | I (energy full) | Numpad 5 |
| Pause | Esc | — |

### Acceptance Criteria (15 checks)
Launch (3), Movement (2), Combat (4), Round (3), Ultimate (3)

### MVP Playable Loop
Launch → P1/P2 Spawn → Fight → HP → 0 → Round End → Best of 3 → Match End → Restart
Every node must have a verifiable pass condition.
