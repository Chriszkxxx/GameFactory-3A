# Mechanic Code Generation Requirements — gameForge_fight

## Overview
Generate complete UE5 gameplay logic (Blueprints or C++ module) for the 3D
one-on-one boxing/fighting game. The generated code receives character assets,
animation clips, scene, and VFX as input, then produces playable gameplay.

## Engine
- **Engine**: Unreal Engine 5
- **Module Name**: FightArenaGameplay

## Input Assets
- **Character Assets**: warrior (武士), armored_goddess (铠甲女神)
- **Animation Clips**: 5 core animations per character (10 total)
  - idle, move, punch, ultimate, hit_react
- **Scene**: Sicka Yazd medieval exterior map (imported open-source)
- **VFX Assets**: fire_punch_uppercut (flame particles for ultimate)
- **Engine API Docs**: agent_skills/engine_context/ue5_api.md

## Required Systems

### 1. FightCharacterController
Character control for movement, attack, and block.
- Horizontal movement (A/D, ←/→)
- Depth movement (W/S, ↑/↓) — limited to ~5 meter range
- Character always faces opponent
- Punch input → play punch animation → activate hitbox during active frames
- Block input → reduce incoming damage
- Hit reaction triggered by combat system
- Ultimate activation when energy is full

### 2. FightInputHandler
Input routing for P1 (keyboard) and P2 (keyboard or AI).
- P1: A/D (move), W/S (depth), J (punch), U (block), I (ultimate), Esc (pause)
- P2: ←/→ (move), ↑/↓ (depth), Numpad 1 (punch), Numpad 4 (block), Numpad 5 (ultimate)
- Routes input to the correct FightCharacterController
- Supports AI takeover for P2

### 3. FightCombatSystem
Hit detection, damage calculation, and hitstun frames.
- Hitbox/hurtbox overlap detection during attack active frames
- Fighter health: 100 HP
- Damage values:
  - Punch: 5 damage, low hitstun
  - Ultimate: 25 damage, launch effect with fire VFX
- Energy charge: each landed punch adds 10 energy; blocked punches add 0
- Ultimate available at 100 energy
- Hitstun duration prevents action during reaction

### 4. FightRoundManager
Round management: timer, win/loss, best-of-3.
- Round timer: 99 seconds countdown
- Round ends on K.O. (HP = 0) or timer expiry
- Timer expiry: fighter with more HP wins the round
- Best of 3: first to win 2 rounds wins the match
- Round transitions: "Round X", "FIGHT!", "K.O.", "You Win/Lose"
- Reset characters to start positions between rounds
- Match end screen with winner announcement

### 5. FightCameraController
Side-view camera following the midpoint of both fighters.
- Fixed side angle
- Follows midpoint between P1 and P2
- Zoom adjustment based on distance between fighters
- Smooth interpolation, no jitter

### 6. FightUltimateSkill
Ultimate skill system with energy charge and fire VFX.
- Energy fills from landing punches (10 per hit, blocked = 0)
- Ultimate available at 100 energy
- Activation: brief slow-motion, fire VFX on fist
- Fire Punch Uppercut: flaming uppercut dealing 25 damage
- Both characters use the same ultimate mechanic
- Energy resets to 0 after use

### 7. FightAIBehavior
AI opponent behavior for P2.
- States: idle, approach, attack, retreat, ultimate
- Decision based on distance, HP, energy:
  - Far: approach
  - Close: attack or block
  - Low HP: retreat or block
  - Full energy: use ultimate
- Reaction time delay (0.3-0.6 seconds) for fairness

## Controls

| Action | P1 | P2 |
|--------|----|----|
| Move | A / D | ← / → |
| Move Depth | W / S | ↑ / ↓ |
| Punch | J | Numpad 1 |
| Block | U | Numpad 4 |
| Ultimate | I (when energy full) | Numpad 5 |
| Pause | Esc | — |

## Acceptance Criteria
- Compile success (no errors)
- Characters can move with A/D and arrow keys
- Characters face each other at all times
- Punch deals damage to opponent
- Hit reaction animations play on hit
- Energy bar fills from landing punches
- Ultimate triggers fire VFX and deals damage
- Timer counts down from 99
- K.O. ends round when HP reaches 0
- Best of 3, first to 2 wins works correctly
- HP bars update in real time
- Round indicator updates across rounds
- FIGHT text appears at round start
- AI opponent makes decisions and fights
- Camera tracks both fighters
- Press Enter on match end to restart

## Validation Checks
1. compile_success — Game compiles without errors
2. character_can_move — Characters respond to movement input
3. animation_can_play — All 5 core animations per character play
4. combat_deals_damage — Punches reduce opponent HP
5. block_reduces_damage — Blocking mitigates damage
6. round_timer_works — Timer counts down from 99
7. ko_detected — Round ends on HP = 0
8. best_of_three — Match ends after 2 round wins
9. energy_fills — Energy bar accumulates during combat
10. ultimate_triggers — Ultimate activates fire VFX and damage
11. ui_updates — HP bars, timer, round indicator update
12. ai_works — AI opponent makes decisions and fights
13. camera_follows — Camera tracks both fighters
